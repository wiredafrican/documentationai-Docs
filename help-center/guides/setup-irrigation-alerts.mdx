---
title: Setting Up Irrigation Alerts
description: Get notified about irrigation needs in Applicious.
---

## Overview

Applicious lets you set irrigation alerts based on soil data.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Alerts">
    In the Applicious dashboard, select **Alerts** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create new alert">
    Click **Add Alert** and choose **Irrigation** as the type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set conditions">
    Choose a soil moisture threshold (e.g., below 30%).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Confirm to start receiving notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>
