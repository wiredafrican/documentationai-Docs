## Adding a New Farm

1. Log in to your Applicious dashboard.
2. Click on **Farms** in the sidebar.
3. Press **Add Farm** button.
4. Enter your farm name and location.
5. Save your changes.

## Note

You must be an administrator to add new farms.

```tip
Add field boundaries and crops after your farm is created for best results.
```
