---
title: Device Not Connecting
description: Fix hardware connection issues in Applicious.
---

## Symptoms

- Device is offline in dashboard
- Not receiving field sensor data

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check power supply">
    Ensure the device is plugged in and power LED is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check network">
    Confirm Wi-Fi or SIM card signal is available near the device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart device">
    Unplug power for 10 seconds, then reconnect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If still not connecting, contact Applicious support.
